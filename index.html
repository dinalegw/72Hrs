How it works

Four steps.
No exceptions.

01

Upload Evidence

Video, audio, documents, photos. Everything is encrypted on your device before it ever leaves your hands. We store the cipher — not the content.

02

Set Your Timer

Default is 72 hours. You can go up to 30 days. The clock starts the moment your vault is activated. It does not wait for permission.

03

Check In With Your Phrase

Enter your secret phrase to reset the clock. Web, mobile, SMS, or email — your choice. Only your phrase can reset it. Not a password recovery. Not support. Nobody.

04

If Zero — It Publishes

Your evidence hits the 72Hours public feed, your chosen contacts, and connected platforms simultaneously. Unstoppable. Irreversible. Public forever.

Security model

We can't
stop it either.

Client-Side Encryption

Files are encrypted on your device using AES-256-GCM before upload. Your encryption key is derived from your secret phrase, which never leaves your device. We receive encrypted bytes. Nothing more.

No Secret Storage

Your secret phrase is never stored — only a salted SHA-256 hash used to verify check-ins. There is no "forgot my phrase" flow. This is intentional. Recovery would require storing it. We don't.

Autonomous Publish Trigger

The publish job is a scheduled cron — it does not require a human to approve or trigger. Even if our team were unavailable, arrested, or coerced, the timer would still fire.

Distributed & Open Source

The entire codebase is open source. Anyone can audit it. Publish targets span multiple platforms and jurisdictions — a court order against one node doesn't stop the others. The architecture is built to resist suppression.
